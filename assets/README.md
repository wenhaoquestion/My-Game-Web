# Artwork

Created with the built-in OpenAI image generation tool for this redesign, then encoded with `cwebp`. No remote asset service is used at runtime.

- `snake-feature.webp`: featured banner, quality 84.
- `game-covers.webp`: a four-column, three-row cover atlas, quality 85. `catalog.js` assigns the twelve positions. CSS crops each scene without stretching its proportions.
- `favicon.svg`: native vector pixel mark, matching the header.

## Feature prompt

Use case: stylized-concept. Production background art for the featured banner of Wenhao's Arcade. Wide 3:1 landscape image. Exact scene: glossy translucent jade/lime green voxel snake winding on a near black green square-grid floor, with an expressive simple cuboid head and two inset dark eyes, a single small glowing coral-red food cube near head. Snake entirely in right 60 percent, its body curls into distance right. Left 40 percent empty extremely dark #0b100d background for native HTML copy overlay. Premium minimalist 3D studio render, crisp edges, beautiful restrained lime highlights and soft shadows, isometric perspective camera. Dark background, no text, no UI, no frames, no labels. Match clean premium gaming game-library concept, no neon city, no clutter.

Overall landscape image 2048x1152, each tile 512x384. Each tile independent complete centered 3D mini-scene. Premium polished restrained cinematic 3D studio art, dark charcoal backgrounds, jewel colors. Row3: 9 black and ivory gomoku stones on natural wood intersection grid; 10 circular Chinese chess pieces red 帥 and black 將 on maple board; 11 ace spade and king heart playing cards with poker chips on dark green felt; 12 cyan numeric grid puzzle 1 4 5 outlined rectangular selection glowing gently. Ensure exact regular 4x3 layout all twelve equal sized scenes recognizable at small cover size. No external text or decorative labels. No typography outside intrinsic game numbers.
